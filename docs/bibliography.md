# Scientific bibliography

This is the human-readable bibliography for GitHub readers; structured metadata lives in [references.bib](references.bib). In-text citation style is **author-year**, with linked entries. Source-code attribution and licensing remain in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Januario et al. (2016)

Tiago Januario, Sebastián Urrutia, Celso C. Ribeiro and Dominique de Werra (2016). *Edge coloring: A natural model for sports scheduling*. European Journal of Operational Research, 254(1), 1–8. DOI: [10.1016/j.ejor.2016.03.038](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejor.2016.03.038). Citation key: `januario2016`.

## Urrutia et al. (2021)

Sebastián Urrutia, Dominique de Werra and Tiago Januario (2021). *Recoloring subgraphs of K₂ₙ for sports scheduling*. Theoretical Computer Science, 877, 36–45. DOI: [10.1016/j.tcs.2021.03.029](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2021.03.029). Citation key: `urrutia2021`.

## Ribeiro et al. (2023)

Celso C. Ribeiro, Sebastián Urrutia and Dominique de Werra (2023). *A tutorial on graph models for scheduling round-robin sports tournaments*. International Transactions in Operational Research, 30(6), 3267–3295. DOI: [10.1111/itor.13290](https://doi.org/10.1111/itor.13290). Citation key: `ribeiro2023`.

## Matsumoto and Nishimura (1998)

Makoto Matsumoto and Takuji Nishimura (1998). *Mersenne Twister: A 623-dimensionally equidistributed uniform pseudo-random number generator*. ACM Transactions on Modeling and Computer Simulation, 8(1), 3–30. Citation key: `matsumoto1998`.

## Durstenfeld (1964)

Richard Durstenfeld (1964). *Algorithm 235: Random permutation*. Communications of the ACM, 7(7), 420. DOI: [10.1145/364520.364540](https://doi.org/10.1145/364520.364540). Citation key: `durstenfeld1964`.

## Knuth (1998)

Donald E. Knuth (1998). *The Art of Computer Programming, Volume 3: Sorting and Searching*, 2nd edition. Addison-Wesley. Citation key: `knuth1998`.
